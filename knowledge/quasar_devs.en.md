## Track 1: Software development (section #software)
From prototype to production: fast interfaces, secure APIs and AI assistants connected to your data.
- Frontend: websites, landing pages, web apps and mobile apps that are fast, accessible and custom designed. Tech: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Flutter.
- Backend & APIs: services, databases and integrations (authentication, email, payments and existing systems). Tech: Node.js, SQL, Firestore, Supabase. C# / .NET projects are covered with a partner team.
- Full stack apps: dashboards, client portals and internal tools deployed to the cloud (Next.js, Node.js, C# / .NET, Google Cloud, AWS, Azure).
- Chatbots & AI: assistants that answer with the company's own information (RAG) for customer service, inventory lookups and internal support. Tech: Python, RAG, LLM APIs. (Randall is an example of this service.)

## Track 2: Supply chain consulting (section #supply-chain)
Eight years designing and improving logistics operations, from space planning to the technology that moves it.
- Warehouse, storage, cross dock and distribution center (DC) layouts: storage capacity, product flows, docks, picking zones and future growth; deliverables ready for decisions and investment.
- Process optimization: assessment of receiving, putaway, picking and shipping; bottleneck detection and measurable improvements with KPIs.
- WMS implementation: selection, configuration and integration of the warehouse management system, with testing and training for go-live.
- Sorters & conveyors: sizing and specification of sortation and conveying systems for high volumes of parcels and cases.
- AS/RS & new technologies: automation assessment (AS/RS, shuttles, robotics) with return-on-investment analysis.
- Dashboards & analytics: operations dashboards with the client's own data (Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Firebase).

## How we work (section #proceso)
1. Assessment: a call to understand the operation or idea and define scope.
2. Proposal: a clear plan, timeline and cost before starting.
3. Implementation: delivery in stages with progress you can see and test.
4. Launch & support: go-live, training and follow-up after launch.
