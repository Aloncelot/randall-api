### Software development (#software)
From prototype to production: fast interfaces, secure APIs and AI assistants connected to your data.
- **Frontend**: Fast, accessible websites, landing pages, web apps and mobile apps with custom design. (Technologies / topics: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Flutter)
- **Backend and APIs**: Services, databases and integrations that keep your operation running: authentication, email, payments and existing systems. If your stack is C# / .NET, we cover it with a partner team. (Technologies / topics: Node.js, SQL, Firestore, Supabase, C# / .NET)
- **Full stack applications**: Complete custom systems: dashboards, customer portals and internal tools, deployed to the cloud. (Technologies / topics: Next.js, Node.js, C# / .NET, Google Cloud, AWS, Azure)
- **Chatbots and AI**: Assistants that answer using your company's information (RAG): customer support, inventory lookups and internal help desks. (Technologies / topics: Python, RAG, LLM APIs)

### Supply chain consulting (#supply-chain)
Eight years designing and improving logistics operations: from space planning to the technology that moves it.
- **Warehouse, cross dock and DC layouts**: Layout design and generation: storage capacity, product flows, docks, picking zones and future growth. Deliverables ready to support decisions and investment. (Technologies / topics: Warehouses, Cross docks, Distribution centers)
- **Process optimization**: Diagnosis of receiving, storage, picking and shipping. We find bottlenecks and define measurable improvements. (Technologies / topics: Diagnosis, KPIs, Flows)
- **WMS and equipment tenders**: We support you through tenders for WMS, forklifts, storage systems and other logistics services: requirements, vendor evaluation and follow-up through delivery and go-live. (Technologies / topics: Tender, Vendors, Go-live)
- **Sorters and conveyors**: Sizing and specification of sortation and conveyor systems for high volumes of parcels and cartons, as the basis for their tender. (Technologies / topics: Sorters, Conveyors, Throughput)
- **AS/RS and new technologies**: Automation assessment (AS/RS, shuttles, robotics) with return analysis to decide whether it pays off and when, and support through go-live. (Technologies / topics: AS/RS, Automation, ROI)
- **Dashboards and analytics**: Operations dashboards built on your own data so you can decide with up-to-date information. (Technologies / topics: Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Firebase)
